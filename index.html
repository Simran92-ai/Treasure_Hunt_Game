INSTRUCTIONS

Controls

W/↑ Up   S/↓ Down   A/← Left   D/→ Right   M Switch World (Mirror map)   P/ESC Pause   V 2D/3D view   Q/E Rotate camera (3D; drag & wheel also work)

Objective

Explore the map, collect keys and clues, avoid hazards, unlock restricted areas and reach the treasure before time runs out.

Collectibles

Coins (+10), Clues (+50), Keys (+100, unlock doors), Checkpoints (+75, saves your respawn point).

Hints

Spend score on four hint tiers: Basic Direction (-50), Navigation (-100), Advanced Path (-150), Key/Clue Location (-100). Score never goes below zero and hints have a short cooldown.

Computer Opponent

In Compete mode a real A* pathfinding opponent races you to the treasure. It respects walls, doors and objectives — it never teleports or cheats.

Enhanced 3D View

Press V, the 3D button in-game, or the Settings toggle to switch to a realistic 3D view: PBR materials, real-time shadows, dynamic lights, fog, water and bloom. Pick Low / Medium / High quality in Settings.

Levels

Every map has its own obstacles and monsters, different for Easy, Medium and Hard. Higher difficulty means bigger maps, denser obstacles, more and faster monsters — and some hunters that chase you when they see you.

CG Concepts Box

A live panel shows the computer graphics concepts in use (transformations, matrices, clipping, Bresenham, shadow maps, frustum culling...) with real-time values. Hide it in Settings.

Lives & Health

Hazard tiles cost 25 health. At 0 health you lose a life and respawn at your last checkpoint. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over.
